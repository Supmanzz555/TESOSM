## About

TESOSM is a large-scale kitchen sink modpack focused on exploration, progression, and challenging combat.

The world is designed to remain dangerous throughout the entire playthrough. As players become stronger, the threats they encounter evolve alongside them. Exploration, preparation, and combat mastery are intended to be the primary drivers of progression rather than following a strict questline or heavily gated progression system.

Examples of enhanced difficulty include:

* Creepers capable of breaching defenses.
* More dangerous and intelligent hostile mobs.
* Harder exploration and dungeon encounters.
* Scaling combat difficulty.
* Numerous bosses and endgame threats.

The overall goal is to create a world that rewards preparation and exploration while maintaining a sense of danger from the first day to the final boss.

## Design Philosophy

TESOSM is **not** intended to be a progression-gated modpack.

While some recipes may be adjusted for balance or progression purposes, the pack does not aim to force players through a predetermined path. Players are encouraged to interact with content however they choose, following Minecraft's traditional sandbox philosophy.

Unlike many modern progression packs, TESOSM is not built around:

* Strict quest-gated advancement
* Mandatory progression chains
* Extensive recipe overhauls
* Forced mod progression


TESOSM is designed to remain challenging throughout the entire game.

The goal is not to make the player weak forever, nor is it to create artificial difficulty through excessive grind or progression gates. Instead, the pack aims to maintain a sense of danger and meaningful progression from early game to endgame.

as hostile mobs become stronger, players should also gain access to stronger tools, equipment, abilities, and strategies. Difficulty is intended to scale alongside player power rather than simply overwhelming the player with increasingly unfair encounters.

players are expected to become powerful over time. Becoming overpowered is part of the Minecraft modded experience and is not something TESOSM seeks to completely remove. However, the world should continue presenting new threats, challenges, and encounters that encourage players to make use of that power.

There may be **exceptionally powerful items**, equipment, or endgame rewards that significantly exceed the normal power curve. These are intentional and serve as long-term goals for dedicated players.

Large-scale progression systems and recipe rewrites are currently not a priority and may remain limited throughout the project's lifetime.

## System Requirements

### Recommended Java Version

* Java 25 (64-bit)

While other modern Java versions may function correctly, Java 25 is the primary version used during development and testing.

### Minimum Recommended

* 6 GB RAM allocated to Minecraft
* Modern CPU (mine is Ryzen 7 5800h for references with my friend using intel i7 2600k)
* SSD storage
* Dedicated GPU optional

### Recommended

* 8–12 GB RAM allocated
* Fast CPU with strong single-core performance
* SSD or NVMe SSD
* Dedicated GPU if using shaders

### Multiplayer / Server Hosting

For multiplayer servers:

| Players | Recommended RAM |
| ------- | --------------- |
| 2–4     | 8–10 GB         |
| 5–10    | 10–16 GB        |
| 10+     | 16 GB+          |

Actual requirements will vary depending on player activity, automation, world generation, and loaded dimensions.

### Performance Notes

* CPU performance is one of the most important factors for smooth gameplay.
* SSD or NVMe storage is heavily recommended.
* A powerful GPU is not required for normal gameplay.
* A dedicated GPU is recommended if shaders are enabled.
* Exploration-heavy playthroughs will generate large amounts of terrain and increase storage usage over time.

## Installation

TESOSM is currently in active development and is not yet considered ready for a public release.

At this time, there are no plans to publish the pack on CurseForge or Modrinth. Many gameplay systems, balance adjustments, and progression elements are still being developed, and the overall experience is not yet representative of the intended final.

The pack is currently maintained primarily for personal use and testing among a small group of players. Public distribution may be considered in the future once the pack reaches a more complete and stable state.
